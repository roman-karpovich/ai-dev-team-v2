# Semantic boundaries

Load this reference only when acceptance turns on an input-domain boundary or
when a removed, deprecated, or unavailable upstream value is replaced by a
local derivation.

## Accepted domain

Record the accepted input domain, verified domain invariants, and explicitly
unsupported inputs that affect acceptance. Owner authority defines product
scope; it does not prove a factual invariant. Verify discoverable invariants
from repository or applicable platform evidence.

A constructible out-of-domain counterexample does not enlarge the contract.
If domain membership is material and unresolved, inspect available evidence or
ask one focused owner decision before adding defensive behavior. A blocking
review finding must identify a reachable trigger inside the accepted domain
or a contradicted boundary. Record a credible but unresolved domain concern
as a non-blocking observation or open question instead of dropping it.

For a counted cold path, the cold-review contact boundary overrides this
owner-decision route. Record unresolved domain membership as a terminal gap or
`HOLD` without contacting the owner or launcher. Outside a counted cold path,
the ordinary owner-decision route above still applies.

## Upstream replacement

When local logic replaces an upstream value, establish semantic equivalence
independently of the candidate formula and tests. Determine what the upstream
value included and excluded from authoritative specification, source, or
targeted history. Tests that mirror the derivation do not prove equivalence.

If equivalence is unknown or semantics differ, pause development or withhold
review acceptance unless the owner explicitly approves the semantic change.
Ordinary local calculations and refactors do not trigger this rule when they
do not replace an upstream value.

Treat sibling repositories, ignored symlink targets, deployment snapshots, and
runtime configuration outside the bound worktree as external operational
evidence. Record provenance and freshness; do not present them as verified
facts from the task snapshot.
